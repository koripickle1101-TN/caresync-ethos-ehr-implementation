# LinkedIn Caption

I created CareSync Ethos as a Health IT implementation use case focused on patient safe digital change.

The project explores how a regional health care organization can replace an outdated charting system with a new EHR without rushing implementation, increasing provider burnout, or leaving patients behind.

My focus was on the leadership side of Health IT: workflow testing, medication safety, frontline adoption, equitable patient access, phased rollout, and trust centered implementation.

Core principle:

Technology should earn trust before it asks people to depend on it.
