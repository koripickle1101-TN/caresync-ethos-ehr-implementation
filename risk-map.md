# Implementation Risk Map

## CareSync Ethos

A rushed EHR launch creates risk in five major areas.

## Risk Area 1

Medication Safety

Potential Failure

Medication lists, allergy information, dosage instructions, or reconciliation workflows may not transfer accurately.

Operational Impact

Clinicians may make decisions using incomplete or unreliable information.

Patient Impact

Patients may experience medication errors, confusion, delays, or loss of trust.

Prevention Strategy

Test medication reconciliation workflows before go live, validate migrated data, involve pharmacists and nurses in pilot testing, and create a clear escalation pathway for medication discrepancies.

## Risk Area 2

Provider Burnout

Potential Failure

The system may increase clicks, duplicate documentation, inbox burden, after hours charting, and alert fatigue.

Operational Impact

Staff may create workarounds, resist adoption, or lose trust in the system.

Patient Impact

Patients may feel rushed, unheard, or disconnected during visits.

Prevention Strategy

Use role based training, protected learning time, super users, usability feedback loops, and post launch burden monitoring.

## Risk Area 3

Patient Access

Potential Failure

Patients may struggle with portal setup, language barriers, disability access, mobile usability, internet access, or digital literacy.

Operational Impact

Staff may receive more phone calls, manual requests, and portal troubleshooting tasks.

Patient Impact

Patients may miss messages, appointments, test results, or care instructions.

Prevention Strategy

Offer plain language instructions, interpreter support, mobile friendly tools, phone support, in person help, and alternative non digital access pathways.

## Risk Area 4

Workflow Disruption

Potential Failure

Real care processes may not match the system build.

Operational Impact

Staff may rely on side spreadsheets, verbal workarounds, duplicate entry, and manual reconciliation.

Patient Impact

Care delays, communication gaps, and documentation errors may increase.

Prevention Strategy

Map real workflows before build decisions, include frontline users in design, and test high risk workflows with realistic scenarios.

## Risk Area 5

Trust Collapse

Potential Failure

Staff may stop trusting the system if early errors are ignored or minimized.

Operational Impact

Shadow systems, manual double checking, and informal workarounds may increase.

Patient Impact

Care becomes less consistent and less reliable across departments.

Prevention Strategy

Treat user feedback as a safety signal, monitor adoption behavior, track workaround patterns, and respond quickly to reported problems.
