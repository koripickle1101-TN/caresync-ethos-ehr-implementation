# Implementation Scorecard

## CareSync Ethos

This scorecard measures whether the EHR implementation is safe, usable, inclusive, reliable, and trusted.

## Metric Category

Patient Safety

What to Measure

Medication reconciliation errors, allergy alert issues, duplicate patient records, downtime events, safety reports, and documentation defects.

Why It Matters

These indicators show whether the system is protecting patients or creating preventable risk.

## Metric Category

Staff Adoption

What to Measure

Training completion, super user feedback, help desk ticket volume, workaround reports, user satisfaction, and after hours documentation.

Why It Matters

Adoption determines whether the system becomes trusted infrastructure or another source of operational burden.

## Metric Category

Equitable Access

What to Measure

Portal activation by age group, language access requests, mobile usability issues, call volume, accessibility complaints, and in person support requests.

Why It Matters

Digital transformation fails ethically if it improves access for some patients while leaving others behind.

## Metric Category

Workflow Reliability

What to Measure

Scheduling errors, message routing delays, care coordination defects, handoff breakdowns, duplicate documentation, and manual reconciliation volume.

Why It Matters

Reliable workflows protect both patient safety and staff capacity.

## Metric Category

Trust Stability

What to Measure

Shadow spreadsheets, manual double checking, bypass behavior, verbal workarounds, escalation spikes, and repeated complaints from the same workflow area.

Why It Matters

Trust collapse is an operational failure signal. When people stop trusting the system, manual compensation grows quickly.
